Google published these 40 sounds on the Material Design "Sound resources" page,
"Available under CC-BY 4.0":
https://web.archive.org/web/20190717142129/https://material.io/design/sound/sound-resources.html

Google's own download link no longer works, so these OGG files come from the
copy of the original zip kept at
https://archive.org/details/material-design-sound-resources
(folder material_product_sounds/ogg). Each file matches the MD5 checksum listed
for it there. They are unchanged.

When you share a sound made from these, credit "Material Design product sounds
by Google, CC BY 4.0" and say that you changed it.
